In the world of gerrymandering, there are many reasons to want to take a ‘representative sample’ of the space of balanced graph partitions—that is, a sample pulled from a known distribution which is clearly relevant to the political districting problem. There are numerous Markov chain methods, plus sequential Monte Carlo, plus a direct tree decomposition method, at least. I’ll discuss this modeling problem at a high level, and will also offer some tools and diagnostics for comparisons.
